Mission

OPEN SOURCE • HUMAN FIRST

Mobility should never be expensive.

Open Source Prosthetics develops open hardware, shares printable prosthetic designs, and enables communities around the world to manufacture life-changing devices locally.

Open Hardware

100% Open Designs

Every prosthetic is designed to be improved, adapted and shared by the global maker community.

Distributed Manufacturing

Print Anywhere

Using affordable 3D printers, prosthetics can be manufactured close to those who need them.

Engineering for humanity.

Millions of people live without access to affordable prosthetics. Our mission is to remove cost as a barrier through open-source engineering, distributed manufacturing and global collaboration.
